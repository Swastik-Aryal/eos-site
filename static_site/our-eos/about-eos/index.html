Eos Advisors

Eos Advisors is a Boutique Advisory Firm, providing specialized tailor-made services for clients in the areas of Private Business Consulting, Equity Advisory, Development Consulting & Investment Advisory in Nepal.

Our pièce de résistance, is our outstanding Financial Consulting portfolio, where we provide our clients with an absolutely customizable set of services. Having worked with some of Nepal’s largest companies in major business sectors such as Telecommunications, Renewable Energy, Tourism & Hospitality and Healthcare, our small of group of highly skilled professionals possess an ocean of experience, available at your disposal to help you make, as well as execute the best business decision!

What We Do!

Eos Advisor’s main focus is to develop Nepal’s Financial Sector, particularly its Equity Market by working to facilitate Foreign Direct Investment. We strive to not only directly support enterprises in Nepal but to also assist foreign companies, agencies and foundations looking to do business in Nepal, as well as contribute to its overall economic development.

Our expertise however is not limited by our scope of focus. Apart from our Financial Consulting Services, Eos Advisors collaborates closely with a range of partners that include expert advisors and consultants, hailing from various other technical and business fields, to provide our clients with the right set of services they require.

We strive to assist our clients to anticipate opportunities, as well as comprehend the risk of their business investments from a holistic stand point, covering all areas from potential legal issues, to financial, commercial and secretarial pitfalls, as well advising our clients how to best optimize their investments for the future!
